How Color Theory Plays a Role in the Visual Appeal of a Website

Everyday we frequently visit different or the same websites but have you ever stopped to wonder why these sites that you frequent daily are so visually appealing? If so, you're in luck, because this article is going to demonstrate the role color theory plays in two different websites by comparing them. Color theory can be defined as the study of how colors interact with each other and the effects they have on human perception and emotions.

The two websites that will be compared are Deadline and AnimeNewsNetwork let's get started!

Deadline

AnimeNewsNetwork

Both websites make excellent use of whitespace because visually both of the websites are easy on the eyes and are not overstimulating. Whitespace can be defined as the areas on the page that have blank space or are unmarked. The use of whitespace helps to create clear focal points that direct the eye's attention.

The color palatte that the Deadline webiste goes for is a palatte that uses more neutral colors such as black and gray. Neutral colors are colors that cannot be found on the color wheel and they tend to be absent of color. The Deadline website also uses the color red which is a primary color. The color wheel is a visual representation of colors and their relationships to each other. It is used to understand how colors can be combined to create new colors and how they can be used together to create a harmonious design. In this case, the color palatte that the AnimeNewsNetwork website uses consists mostly of cool colors such as blue and green. Cool colors help to convey a sense of calm. Blue is also a primary color along with red and yellow, which means that it cannot be created by mixing other colors together. Green is a secondary color which means that it can be created by mixing two primary colors together. For example, blue and yellow. Also, unlike the Deadline website, the AnimeNewsNetwork website uses different tints of the color blue as well. A tint is created by mixing a color or hue with white. Lastly, both websites have color harmony which means that they show unity between the colors presented.

In conclusion, both websites make excellent use of color theory to create visually appealing designs.
